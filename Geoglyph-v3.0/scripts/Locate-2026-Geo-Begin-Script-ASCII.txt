LOCATE 2026 - BEGIN GEOGLYPH REHEARSAL
======================================

Start with the Survey123 command Begin Geoglyph. The control panel hides
and playback prepares the fonts and morphs before the first cue.

Map messages are indented under their labels. Line breaks are
intentional. The \u{...} notation represents Unicode characters in this
ASCII transcript. Cue holds begin after the actions and any morph in that
cue finish. The opening camera flight runs across several cues.

Playback source
apps/geoglyph/src/scripts/locate-2026-rehearsal.json

CONFERENCE DAY OPENING
----------------------

01  conference-day
    Place Geo beside the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre in South Wharf.
    Set the anchor to latitude -37.82402 and longitude 144.95604.
    Show in 2D using Segoe UI Emoji in native colour (300 m high).
    Map message (sleeping face)
      \u{1F634}
    White top overlay in Gill Sans Ultra Bold Condensed - "Conference day..."
    Start the 20-second camera flight to Geo as the opening title appears.
    Hold for 10 seconds.

02  approach
    Clear the white overlay.
    Wait for the camera to arrive before the yawn cue.
