The original SID program bytes are bundled in launcher/sid-tracks.js using
base64 for direct-from-disk loading. Playback executes the PSID and RSID
programs through local C64 emulators.

Music copyrights remain with their respective rights holders. The above SID
credit fields identify the supplied files; they do not establish current rights
ownership or grant a licence. These tunes are included for the requested private
evaluation. The emulator licences cover their own code. Any public/commercial
deployment needs the relevant rights-holder permissions.
HVSC copyright information: https://www.hvsc.c64.org/info

Emulator: Hermit jsSID 0.9.1 by Mihaly Horvath (Hermit), 2016.
https://github.com/og2t/jsSID
Adapted CPU/SID source: vendor/sid-core.js
Retained upstream README and licence statement: vendor/jsSID-README.txt
Adaptation: supplied byte-array loading, injected sample rate, isolated state,
explicit 6581 model, deterministic restart, streaming AudioWorklet output and
bounded ScriptProcessor fallback. No tune transcription or replacement music.

RSID emulator: libsidplayfp-wasm 1.1.1, reSIDfp build.
Source and licence details are in vendor/libsidplayfp-NOTICE.md.
